Questions

FAQ

What's the difference between the season price and the prepaid price?

The prepaid price is the lower price, and it applies when you pay in full up front. For example, a standard space is $695 prepaid, or $750 at the season rate. A deposit of half the season price holds a space at the season rate. The balance is due within 30 days or before the map goes to press, whichever comes first. Name listings are paid in full only and don't have a deposit option.

How do I hold a space?

A deposit of half the season price holds a space. Pay in full up front to get the prepaid price. Spaces are first come, first served, and they are limited.

How many maps are printed?

We print 25,000 copies of the illustrated map, plus 5,000 personalized Realty Partner copies with the agency logo on the cover.

What season do these prices cover?

These prices are good for the 2027 season. The next season starts in March 2028.

What do I get online with a print ad?

Every print ad includes one year of online advertising as an Enhanced listing.

What are the online listing options?

Basic is free. Enhanced is $99 a year, and it is included free with any print space. Premium Featured is $299 a year or $29 a month.

What is the Premium Featured Listing?

A new online upgrade: a full business profile with photos, description, hours and website, plus a promotional offer, a subtle Featured badge on your pin and in search, and a spot in the rotating Featured Businesses section coming to the visitor site. It is $299 a year or $29 a month. You can add it on its own or bundle it with a print ad.

How is the back of the map organized?

Spaces are limited. Ads on the back are grouped into five sections: Eat & Drink, Shop, Play & Rentals, Stay & Realty, and Services.

What is a Realty Partner?

There are only four Realty Partner spots, for realty and vacation rental companies. The agency logo replaces the big Holden Beach Insider logo on the front cover of that agency's copies.

How do I see a sample of my ad?

Open the online map, tap your business, then tap “See your sample ad.”

When is my space reserved?

Your space is reserved when your payment goes through. Placement is first come, first served. Premium corners and the Realty Partner panel are limited. Paying by the early-bird deadline locks your placement preference.

How do I send my ad?

After you pay, email your logo and artwork to tourholdenbeach@gmail.com. Finished ads should be a press-ready PDF, CMYK, 300 dpi, built to the final ad size. We'll send ad templates. Don't have an ad? Layout is included: up to two hours for standard spaces, premium corners and Realty Partners, and up to one hour for half spaces. More design time is $75 an hour. All artwork is due February 15, 2027.

My business is already on the sample map. Am I listed?

The pins and directory on the proof are a sample. Final listings on the 2027 map are paid. Use the Claim your listing button on your pin, or pick a name listing or ad space above.

What do I get on the online map?

Every business on the online map has Call and Directions buttons. An Enhanced listing, which is included with any print ad or $99 a year on its own, also adds Website, photo and Deal buttons, and Insiders Club offers. Premium Featured adds a full profile and offer for $299 a year or $29 a month.

Are there multi-year discounts?

Yes. A two-season agreement takes 10% off 2027 and locks your rate for 2028. A three-season agreement takes 15% off 2027 and locks your rate for 2028 and 2029. Discounts don't combine, so the single best one applies. Contact us to set one up.

Will featured businesses crowd out everyone else?

No. The map stays a useful local directory, not a billboard. Every business keeps the same pin and the same place in search. Featured listings add a small badge and a fuller profile; they don't hide or push down anyone else.

Can I cancel?

Space can't be canceled within 60 days of the press date. Email us as early as possible if your plans change.

Is tax included?

No. Prices do not include applicable tax.
